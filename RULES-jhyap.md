# Jhyap (Nepal's Yaniv) - rules as built

Researched 24 Oct 2026. "Jhyap" is the Nepali name of Yaniv (Israel) - also called Dhumbal in Nepal.

## Sources
1. pagat.com - https://www.pagat.com/draw/yaniv.html ("Yaniv / Jhyap / Dhumbal"): base rules plus a "Nepal" variant paragraph.
2. Wikipedia - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yaniv_(card_game) (lists "Jhyap" as an alias): same base rules, different wording on a few details.
3. Play Store search for Jhyap: only a meme-sound app (com.aeroxstudio.jhyaap) and an Israeli Yaniv app; no Nepali app description with rules could be found, and no Nepali blog/YouTube transcript was reachable. So the Nepali evidence is pagat's Nepal paragraph only. Known gap.

## Rules built (2-5 players)
- Deal 5 cards each, one card turned up to start the pile, the rest face down as the stock.
- Card values: Ace 1, 2-10 face value, J, Q and K 10 in every variant (Chairman, 27 Oct 2026: Jack no longer 0). Jokers 0 (international deck only). A call is allowed only with 5 points or less.
- Your turn: either call JHYAP, or throw then draw. You always throw first, then pick up exactly one card.
- What you may throw: one card; or 2+ cards of the same rank; or 3+ cards of the same suit in a run (ace is low only: A-2-3 yes, Q-K-A no). A joker may stand for any card. Only one combination per turn.
- What you may draw: the top of the stock, or a card the previous player threw (Nepal: any one card of their throw; international: only the first or last card of a set/run).
- Call: only at the start of your own turn, with a hand total of 5 or less.
- Call outcome: if the caller has strictly the lowest total, caller scores 0 and everyone else scores their own total. If another player has an equal or lower total ("Asaf"), the caller scores their total plus the penalty, and (international) the others score their totals / (Nepal) the others score nothing.
- The lowest total starts and deals the next round. Stock empty: the pile (except the last throw) is shuffled into a new stock.
- Scores add up round by round; a player over the limit is out, last player standing wins the match.

## Chosen variant and options
| Question | Default (Nepal, pagat) | Option (International, pagat/Wikipedia) |
|---|---|---|
| Deck | 52 cards, no jokers | 54 with 2 jokers worth 0 |
| Jack | 10 points | 10 points |
| Draw from previous throw | any one card of it | first or last card only |
| Failed call penalty | +25, others score 0 | +30, others score their hand |
| Out when total over | 100 | 200 |
| Call limit | 5 (pagat; Wikipedia "often 5") | |
Disagreement noted: pagat says the lowest hand also scores its own total when someone else called; Wikipedia says the round winner scores 0. Built as pagat (winner of the call scores 0 only if the caller; any other lowest hand scores its total) in international mode.
Direction is counter-clockwise in Nepal (pagat); the table is drawn left-to-right, so turn order follows seat numbers. Not built: halving at multiples of 50, three-zeros bonus, slam-down, house rules with call limits above 5.
