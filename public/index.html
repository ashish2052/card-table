    How Marriage works

    • Goal: make 7 melds of 3 cards from your 21, then throw the last card. Draw one (stock or the last discard), throw one.
    • Melds: tunnela (3 identical cards), pure run (3 in a row, one suit, A low or high), set (same rank, 3 suits), or a dirty meld using maal/jokers.
    • See the maal: lay down 3 tunnelas / pure runs. Then you see the tiplu. Wild cards: tiplu, poplu (rank up), jhiplu (rank down), alter (same rank, same colour), jokers (same rank, other colour), man (printed joker).
    • After seeing you can't pick up a discarded wild card. Unseen players pay the winner 10, seen players 3, and everybody pays the difference in maal points.
    • Dublee: identical pairs. 7 dublees win (or 8 on the pagat rule, where 7 lets you see the maal).
    • Controls: tap a card to select, drag to reorder or onto the pile to discard. Tap the stock or the pile to draw.
